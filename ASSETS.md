## Lost in Egypt

Source: https://github.com/fagerhu03/lost_in_egypt/tree/master/assets/screenshot

Home, AI camera, map, community, solo trip and tour images were downloaded from the
repository's `assets/screenshot/*.png` on September 27, 2026. Local `.webp` copies
are lossless conversions at their original 1408 × 2974 dimensions. The pictures
already include a device frame; the gallery deliberately does not add another.
No screen contents were cropped, altered or invented.

## Font and identity

Manrope is locally bundled under the SIL Open Font License. See
`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Static 400/500/600/700/800 instances were produced from the
upstream variable font to make weight selection consistent in Flutter Web.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

The FA monogram is an original vector. The Flutter mark is Flutter's built-in
`FlutterLogo` widget. Omar's portfolio was a visual and interaction reference;
none of its graphics, identity, content or achievements are included.
